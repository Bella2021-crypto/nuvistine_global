Why shop through Nuvistine?

  • We source items in person where possible and check them before dispatch.
  • We share available photos, videos and purchase details for your order.
  • We confirm product details and measurements with you before purchase.
  • You see the item cost, service fee and delivery charges before you pay.
  • Special sourcing, rush requests and alterations can be discussed in advance.

Simple, open fees

Your quote will show the product cost, Nuvistine service fee and delivery separately. Shipping is quoted based on destination, parcel size and weight.

Nigeria & Africa

12%

Of the item cost, minimum ₦3,000 per order. Nigerian customers can pay in naira by bank transfer. Delivery is quoted separately.

International orders

20%

Of the item cost, minimum $30 per order. International shipping with tracking is quoted separately.

Rush orders, tailoring and special handling are quoted separately. Payment is made before purchase using the payment method provided with your quote. Customs duties in the destination country are the buyer's responsibility.
