Why buy through me

  • I shop in person at the Lagos markets, so I see the fabric and stitching before you pay.
  • You get proof for every order: the item at the stall, in hand, the receipt and your tracking number.
  • Sizing is handled with your measurements, and I measure the item before it ships.
  • Tailoring and alterations are available through trusted tailors.
  • You see every cost before you pay. No hidden charges.

Simple, open fees

Fair pricing for everyone, wherever you are. Shipping is always charged at cost, and you see every figure before you pay.
